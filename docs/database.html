Offline data model

Everything needed, stored locally.

The MVP stores operational settings and queue records on the device. It avoids customer names and other unnecessary personal data, while keeping enough timestamps to calculate waiting and service performance.

Business settings

FieldTypePurpose
businessNameStringBusiness name shown in the queue header and digital display.
counterNameStringService counter shown to operators and customers.
operatorNameStringOptional operator identity for the queue header.
address / phoneStringBusiness profile values maintained in Settings.
openingHoursStringReadable opening-hours value for the business profile.

FieldTypePurpose

statusEnumWAITING, SERVING, COMPLETED, or SKIPPED.
createdAtDateTimeCreation timestamp used for waiting time and ordering.
calledAt / startedAtDateTime?Call and service-start timestamps.
completedAt / skippedAtDateTime?Terminal lifecycle timestamp.
lastCalledAtDateTime?Last recall timestamp.
recallCountIntegerNumber of recall announcements for the ticket.

Queue settings

The queue configuration stores prefix, next number, maximum number, number length, reset mode, and feature flags for priority and appointment tickets. The default algorithm pads numbers to three digits and produces A-001 through A-999 before wrapping to the configured starting number.

prefix = A
numberLength = 3
startingNumber = 1
maximumNumber = 999
resetMode = Every Day

Ordering and uniqueness

Waiting tickets are sorted by priority first, regular tickets second, and creation timestamp third. Before a new ticket is persisted, the store checks active records to prevent duplicate queue numbers. Completed and skipped records remain available for history and statistics.

CSV export

The export contains a header and one row per local record. It intentionally omits personal customer data.

queue_number,status,created_at,called_at,completed_at,waiting_minutes,service_minutes
A-021,COMPLETED,08:15,08:21,08:27,6,6

Privacy and offline behavior

The app requires no backend, does not log customer information, and requests no unnecessary permissions. CSV export is handed to the platform share system rather than uploaded by Pocket Queue.
